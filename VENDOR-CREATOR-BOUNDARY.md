# Lead360 vendor and creator boundary

Lead360 is the operating backend for the Kin Marche vendor. It owns vendor-side
catalogue, inventory, stores, pricing, promotions, orders, fulfilment, returns,
refunds, warranties, customer service, content operations, social publishing,
commercial campaigns, team access, reporting, and settings.

Creator self-service remains exclusively in the Stylemint mobile app. Lead360
must not expose creator profile editing, creator account settings, personal
publishing tools, creator onboarding, creator wallet/payout self-service, or the
creator's accept/reject workflow.

Lead360 may expose the vendor side of a creator relationship: finding a partner,
sending an invitation, defining a brief and commission, funding a campaign,
pausing or ending the vendor contract, and reading attributed commercial
performance. The creator receives and acts on those items in the mobile app.

This boundary keeps Lead360 a complete Vendor OS without creating a second,
conflicting creator portal.
